Privacy Policy

Transparency and trust matter to us. Learn how JobFinder Pakistan collects, uses and handles your information.

Last Updated: November 22, 2026
Welcome to JobFinder Pakistan.

This policy explains the information collected through our website, why we collect it, and how you can contact us about your personal information.

01

Information We Collect

When you submit an application, we may collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • Phone number and city
  • Education details
  • Work experience, if provided
  • Uploaded CV or resume
02

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to receive, review and manage job applications and support recruitment-related activities.

03

Data Storage & Security

Application records and CV files are stored using Supabase services. Access to application records is restricted through our administrative system. CV links may be temporary.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

04

Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell applicants' personal information. Authorized administrators and relevant service providers may access information when necessary to operate the website.

05

Third-Party Websites

JobFinder Pakistan may link to external job portals and other websites. These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

06

Cookies & Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve our services. Third-party analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies.

07

Data Deletion Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted application information. We will review your request.

08

Keep Your Information Safe

Please provide only the information requested by the form. Never upload passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

09

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our website or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Have a Privacy Question?

Our contact email is available for privacy-related questions and requests.
